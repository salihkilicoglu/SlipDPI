SlipDPI is a small program that gets past hostname-based blocking in Türkiye without a VPN or tunnel. It only touches the opening packet of connections to the domains on its list.

WHAT HAPPENS DURING SETUP

After the files are copied, a console window opens and does the following:

  1. Your line is tested and a working setting is chosen (2-3 minutes).
  2. The "SlipDPI" service is installed; it starts automatically with Windows.
  3. DNS queries for the listed domains only are sent to encrypted DNS.
  4. If Chrome is installed, "Secure DNS" is turned on by policy.
  5. The result is verified by connecting to blocked and unblocked addresses.

Do not close that window; it closes by itself when it is done.

WHAT CHANGES ON THE SYSTEM

  - The "SlipDPI" service and the WinDivert driver
  - The folder C:\Program Files\SlipDPI
  - A DNS rule for the listed domains (other names stay on the router's DNS)
  - Two policy values for Chrome (Chrome shows "managed by your organization")

All of it is undone with "Settings > Apps > SlipDPI > Uninstall".

GOOD TO KNOW

  - Windows 11, 64-bit is recommended. Administrator rights are required.
  - If another DPI tool is running (GoodbyeDPI, zapret, ByeDPI), close it first.
  - Some antivirus products block the WinDivert driver.
  - The next page asks whether you want a Start menu and a desktop shortcut.
  - After setup, the "SlipDPI" control window shows the status and lets you add or remove sites and stop or start the service. If you chose no shortcut, open it with C:\Program Files\SlipDPI\panel.cmd.
  - The user guide opens from the "Guide" button in the control window.
